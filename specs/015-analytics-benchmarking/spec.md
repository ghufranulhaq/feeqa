# Spec 015: Business Analytics & Competitor Benchmarking

**Status:** Draft · **Phase:** 2 (basic analytics in Phase 1) · **Depends on:** 005, 007, 008, 010, 011
**Client requirements:** F9 Competitor Benchmarking Dashboard (B2B), F2 retention signals for businesses

## 1. Goal

Give businesses insight they can act on: what customers say, how that changes, and how they compare with their industry and named competitors. The focus is on metrics that drive improvement (response, resolution, refunds, durability, topics) rather than vanity counts. All benchmarks are built from public or anonymised aggregate data.

## 2. User Scenarios

1. **Overview.** An Analyst opens the dashboard and sees, for the chosen period and compared with the previous period: Review Score, Trust Index and components, reviews by rating/source/verification, reply rate and time, case metrics, and invitation funnel. Each tile shows its change.
2. **Retention signals.** The Analyst opens "Durability" and sees that 22% of reviews with a 6-month update got worse, mostly on the topic "refunds", while 61% stayed the same.
3. **Industry benchmark.** The Analyst compares their metrics with the **anonymised industry baseline** for "Airlines, UK": median, 25th, and 75th percentile for each metric, and sees they are in the bottom quartile for refund time.
4. **Named competitors.** The Analyst adds up to 5 named competitors and sees a side-by-side table of **public** metrics (the same as the consumer comparison in 009) plus trends over 12 months.
5. **Export.** The Analyst exports the report as CSV and PDF, and schedules a monthly email to the Owner.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### Own analytics
- **FR-015-01** Dashboard metrics for any date range (max 36 months) with a comparison period:
  - review volume by rating, source label, Verified Experience, language, location;
  - Review Score and Trust Index history (008 snapshots);
  - reply rate and median reply time (007);
  - case metrics (010 FR-010-20);
  - invitation funnel by method and template (005);
  - topics and sentiment trends (011, Phase 2);
  - **durability**: distribution of `improved/unchanged/declined` lifecycle signals, and topics tied to declines (003 FR-003-22);
  - Useful votes on the business's reviews;
  - profile views and widget impressions (016).
- **FR-015-02** Every metric can be filtered by location, source label, verification, language, and category question.
- **FR-015-03** Numbers on the dashboard must match the public numbers for the same definitions and period (single metric definitions shared with 008/009/010).
- **FR-015-04** Exports: CSV (raw aggregated series) and PDF (formatted report). Scheduled email reports (weekly/monthly) to chosen members.
- **FR-015-05** Row-level review exports (for the business's own reviews) contain only public review data plus invitation references the business supplied itself. They never contain reviewer emails, proofs, insider identities, or case attachments.

### Industry benchmark
- **FR-015-06** The industry baseline is computed per **category × country** (and per category globally), monthly. It gives the median, P25, and P75 for: Review Score, Trust Index and components, verified %, reply rate, reply time, first case response, resolution rate, resolution rating, refund success, refund time, and durability decline rate.
- **FR-015-07** Anonymity: a baseline is shown only if it includes **≥ 10 businesses**, and no single business contributes more than 25% of the reviews in the underlying sample. Otherwise it falls back to the parent category or global, and says so.
- **FR-015-08** The business's position is shown as a percentile band (bottom quartile, below median, above median, top quartile) per metric.

### Named competitor benchmarking
- **FR-015-09** A business may choose up to **5 competitors** (plan-dependent, 017) from any Businesses on the Platform.
- **FR-015-10** Competitor data must be limited to **publicly visible** metrics and their history (008 snapshots, 009 comparison metrics, public topic summaries). No invitation, private case, or non-public analytics data from competitors may be shown.
- **FR-015-11** Competitors are **not** notified and cannot opt out, because the data is already public. Businesses under Consumer Warning appear with their warning and hidden scores.

### Insights
- **FR-015-12** "What changed" insights (Phase 2): the system highlights the 3 biggest metric or topic movements in the period, with links to supporting reviews. These are labelled AI where generated by AI (P7).
- **FR-015-13** Analytics access is available to Analyst role and above (001). Analytics depth is plan-gated (017). Basic analytics (Review Score history, rating distribution, reply rate, 90-day range) are **free**.

## 4. Edge Cases & Rules

| Case | Rule |
|------|------|
| Date range with no data | Show empty states, not zeros that look like performance. |
| Date range > 36 months, or end before start | Reject with a message. |
| Metric below its minimum sample | Show "Not enough data" (same minimums as 008/010). |
| Business with many locations (> 500) | Location filter is a searchable selector. Aggregation stays within budget (p95 ≤ 3 s for dashboard load). |
| Competitor added then merged or deleted | Remove it from the list and notify the user. |
| Business adds itself as a competitor | Reject. |
| Export > 1M rows | Async generation with email link, split files. |
| Unauthorized: a Responder schedules a report | Allowed (read only) if Analyst-or-above permissions include it. A consumer account calling the endpoint gets 403. |

## 5. Out of Scope

- Customer-level (individual) tracking or CRM features.
- Revenue attribution (the lead-fee attribution in 017 is separate).
- Paid access to competitors' private data (never available).
- Custom SQL/BI access. Enterprise data feeds are in 016.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All FR-015-01 metrics are shown and match public values and the shared definitions (consistency test).
- [ ] Filters and comparison period calculations are correct on fixtures.
- [ ] Baselines follow the anonymity rules (≥ 10 businesses, ≤ 25% dominance) with fallback (tests).
- [ ] Competitor views show only public data (test tries to read private fields).
- [ ] Row-level exports have no personal data beyond FR-015-05 (schema test).
- [ ] Dashboard p95 load ≤ 3 s on the largest fixture business.
